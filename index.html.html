ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Ltd. v. Essar Bulk Terminal Ltd.
2021 INSC 478 | (2022) 1 SCC 712
14-09-2021
2021
 2J
Civil Appeal
 Section 9 & Section 20
Issue: Whether the supervisory court at the seat can finish adjudicating an already-heard Section 9 application after the arbitral tribunal is constituted.
Holding: The bar under Section 9(3) does not oust the jurisdiction of the seat court to pronounce orders on a Section 9 petition already heard and reserved.
Case Explanation: Disputes arose under a cargo handling agreement where the seat court had heard extensive Section 9 interim protection arguments before the tribunal was formally constituted. The Supreme Court clarified that 'entertain' in Section 9(3) means taking up for consideration; once the supervisory court at the seat has applied its mind and reserved orders, it need not relegate parties to Section 17.

 Section 34(2A) & Section 20
